# The first real visitor, without waiting on one admin

Written 2026-09-16 (Pacific). The Let's Go Solo request is out and may never be answered. These are
three other routes that break no rules, cost nothing, use no betting brand account and fake nothing.
Each is ready to execute; each needs one decision or one account from you, said plainly.

## Route 1. Places whose whole purpose is showing something you built

**Hacker News "Show HN", Indie Hackers, and BetaList.** On all three, posting your own project is the
point of the section, not a tolerated exception. Show HN's own guidelines describe it as a place to
share something you've made that people can try. That is exactly what this is.

* **Audience:** builders and early adopters, not travelers. That is fine for the goal. The first
  hundred visitors do not have to be the eventual market; they have to be real people who click,
  look, and tell us what is broken.
* **Risk:** low, provided the post is honest about the size of the network. HN in particular punishes
  hype and rewards "this is small and here is what I learned".
* **Needs from you:** an HN account and an Indie Hackers account, **created by you**. A brand new HN
  account posting a link on its first day is often flagged, so the account should comment on a few
  unrelated threads first. BetaList is a submission form and needs no reputation.
* **Tracked link:** `https://ruinmytrip.com/?utm_source=referral&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=show_hn`
  (and `show_ih`, `betalist`).

**The Show HN post, exact:**

> **Show HN: RuinMyTrip, see which travelers' dates overlap yours in a city**
>
> I built a small site where you post a city and two dates, and you see other travelers whose dates
> cross yours. If you both want to, you connect, and messaging only opens once you've both said yes.
>
> It is honestly empty right now. The interesting problems so far have been the cold start ones: what
> a city page should say when nobody is on it without inventing people, and how to count real visitors
> when the first thousand "sessions" were crawlers and my own test runs. The analytics now separate
> real, self check, automated and uncertain traffic, and the milestone counter reads zero, which is
> the true number.
>
> It works best around events where lots of people land in one city the same week: Oktoberfest,
> Web Summit, Day of the Dead in Oaxaca. I'd value blunt feedback on whether the core idea is useful
> or whether this is a feature looking for a product.

## Route 2. Travellerspoint, the Travel Companions forum

The one general travel forum still alive, with a dedicated companions board. Its rules, read this
session: promotional posts are deleted without warning, **but a website in your profile is explicitly
allowed**, and a post that genuinely answers a question may mention a site.

* **Audience:** real travelers actively looking for companions. The best intent of the three routes.
* **Risk:** low if done properly, immediate removal if not.
* **How:** a personal account answers five real questions over a week **with no link at all**, with
  `ruinmytrip.com` in the profile. Visitors arrive from the profile, which is exactly what the forum
  permits.
* **Needs from you:** a Travellerspoint account created by you, under your own name.
* **Tracked profile link:** `https://ruinmytrip.com/?utm_source=referral&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=travellerspoint`

## Route 3. The Page admin's own network

The Ruin My Trip Page is administered from a personal profile with 91 friends. Sharing the pinned
intro post to that timeline is not spam: it is a person telling people who know them about something
they are involved in. It is the most normal way anything reaches its first visitors.

* **Audience:** friends and family, mostly not about to travel. Low intent, but real and immediate,
  and friends are the people most likely to try it and say honestly that a button is broken.
* **Risk:** none to any group or account. **It is a personal decision**, because it is that person's
  own network and name.
* **Needs from you:** a yes. I will not share to a personal timeline without it.
* **The share caption, exact:**

> Something I've been helping with: a site where you post your travel dates for a city and see who
> else will be there the same days. It's brand new and very quiet, so if you're traveling anywhere
> soon, adding your trip would genuinely help, and so would telling me what's confusing.

* **Tracked link:** the pinned post already carries `utm_campaign=page&utm_content=intro`.

## What is deliberately not a route

* **The Quora persona in this machine's notes.** Answering as an invented person is fake engagement,
  whatever the answer says.
* **The TrustMyRecord and BetLegend accounts**, on every platform.
* **"Promote your business" Facebook groups.** They allow links, but the audience is other people
  posting links, and nobody there is traveling.
* **Buying anything**, including boosted Page posts.
