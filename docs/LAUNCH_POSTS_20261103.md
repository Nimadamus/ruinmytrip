## 1. Indie Hackers, Tue 3 Nov, morning Pacific (status: needs OK + his account)

Rules: no single site wide promo ban; group owners set guidelines, read the group's before posting.
Build story with real numbers and a specific question does well. Reply to every comment that day.

**Title:** My travel site has 1 member after a month. The form asks what went wrong on your trip. What would you change?

> I kept noticing that the most useful thing I read before a trip was someone saying what went badly. Review sites tend to bury that under five star averages, so I built RuinMyTrip.
>
> What is there today:
> * A page for each of 85 cities and 1,378 places. City pages carry a rating we write ourselves, always labelled "Official Review", so a page is not empty before members arrive.
> * A review form that starts with stars and a few lines and asks for an account only at the end.
> * A free map where you tap the countries you have been to and get a link to share.
> * Travel buddies matched by overlapping dates, including a page per cruise line.
> * Hosts & Locals, opened last week: list yourself as a local for a coffee or a walk, or apply to host for free. Nobody can message you until you say yes, and no address is ever stored.
>
> The honest numbers: in the last 31 days about 60 real visitors (after filtering out around 26,000 bot hits), 1 member, 0 member reviews. Google showed us 867 times in October and sent 8 clicks. So reach is the problem, but I would rather hear it from people looking at it cold.
>
> How it is run: I make the product calls; day to day code, content checks and moderation are done with an AI agent (Claude) and reviewed before anything ships.
>
> Three questions:
> 1. In the first ten seconds, is it clear what the site is for?
> 2. Would you write a two line "what went wrong" review? If not, what would make you?
> 3. Is Hosts & Locals a separate product that should be split off?
>
