# The minimum viable account plan

**Nothing has been created.** This is the plan, and it is deliberately two accounts rather than five,
because five dead accounts is a worse signal to a visitor than one live one.

## 1. The first account: TikTok

**Why this one and not a Facebook Page.** Every other platform shows a post to people who already
follow the account. TikTok does not. That is the single structural advantage available to a site with
no audience at all, and it is the only channel on this list where the first post can reach somebody.

A RuinMyTrip Facebook Page, by contrast, would start with zero followers and **cannot post into
travel groups**, which is where the audience actually is. Groups need a personal profile. So the
Facebook work stays with a personal account and no Page is created.

* **Handle:** `ruinmytrip` if free, otherwise `ruinmytripcom`. Not a variation with underscores or
  numbers: an unmemorable handle is worse than a slightly longer one.
* **Display name:** RuinMyTrip
* **Bio:**
  > Find the people going where you're going.
  > Post your dates for a city, see whose overlap.
  > New and honest about it.
* **Link:** `https://ruinmytrip.com/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=profile`
* **Profile photo:** the site mark. No stock photo of a beach.

## 2. The second account: Instagram, and only alongside TikTok

The same footage with a different caption costs about ten minutes more per video, so the marginal
cost is small. On its own it would not be worth it, because Instagram reach without followers is
close to nothing. Same handle, same bio, link
`https://ruinmytrip.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=profile`.

**Create it only after three TikToks are posted.** If nobody will film, neither account is worth
opening.

## 3. Ignore for now

* **X.** Rewards an existing audience more than any platform here. Two posts are written for when
  there is one.
* **A RuinMyTrip Facebook Page.** Cannot post into groups, starts at zero, and would need feeding.
* **YouTube, Pinterest, LinkedIn as a brand.** No audience, no fit, or no time.
* **Reddit as a brand account.** Reddit dislikes brand accounts and the ten posts written for it are
  meant to come from a person.

## 4. The first ten posts for TikTok

All ten are written in full in `docs/ACQUISITION_VIDEO.md` with hooks, on screen text, captions and
tracked links. The order below puts the nearest cohort first and the two honest, non promotional
ones early, because they set what the account is.

| # | Script | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Going to Oktoberfest alone? | Nearest cohort, clearest problem |
| 2 | Would you share an Oktoberfest table with strangers? | Same cohort, different angle |
| 3 | Solo trip, but not alone the whole time | The general case, in one line |
| 4 | Would you meet a traveler whose dates overlap yours? | Asks rather than sells. Sets the tone |
| 5 | The honest empty screen | Shows the real empty product. Nothing else on this list builds more trust |
| 6 | Dia de Muertos, which night | Next cohort, and a genuinely useful warning |
| 7 | The conference app problem | Web Summit, and the sharpest framing we have |
| 8 | Traveling solo to Bangkok? | New Year cohort, largest solo window of the year |
| 9 | Which city is easiest to meet other travelers in? | Broad, shareable, and true |
| 10 | Why the dates matter more than the city | The whole product in twenty five seconds |

## 5. Cadence

**One video a week, Wednesday**, per `docs/ACQUISITION_WEEKLY.md`. Not three a week. A quiet account
that posts one honest thing weekly is easier to sustain and easier to judge than a burst of ten
followed by silence, and ten scripts at one a week is ten weeks of runway.

Reply to comments. Do not follow people, do not comment on other accounts to get attention, and never
send an unsolicited message.

## 6. The metric that decides whether we keep going

**Human visits attributed to `utm_source=tiktok`, read in `/admin/funnel` under "Acquisition, now".**

* **Keep investing:** any video produces 100 views **and** 2 real human visits.
* **Change the approach:** views arrive and visits do not. That is a bio and hook problem, not a
  channel problem, and the fix is the caption rather than the account.
* **Stop:** three videos, no visits at all. The channel is the hypothesis, not the wording, and the
  effort moves to Facebook groups.

**Do not judge it on followers.** A thousand followers who never click is worth less than four
travelers who post dates.

## 7. What this unlocks, and what it does not

Two accounts unlock all ten video scripts and, with a personal Facebook account, fifteen of the
twenty written posts. What no account unlocks is Reddit, which remains the best audience fit and is
blocked both by the network from this machine and by not having a suitable account.
