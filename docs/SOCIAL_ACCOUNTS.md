# The smallest social footprint worth having

**Nothing here has been created.** This is a recommendation and a ranking, and the reason it is short
is that five accounts nobody maintains is worse than one that gets used.

## What exists today, and why none of it fits

| Account | What it is | Usable for RuinMyTrip |
|---|---|---|
| **BetLegend** on X | Sports betting brand | **No.** Off brand, and posting travel content from it risks an unrelated account |
| **TrustMyRecord** Facebook Page | Sports betting brand, and Page access is currently broken | **No.** A Page also cannot post into travel groups; groups need a personal profile |
| **u/TrustMyRecord** on Reddit | Karma 1, betting brand name, last three posts removed by the sitewide spam filter | **No**, and Reddit is blocked from this machine anyway |

## The ranking

### 1. A personal Facebook account, in three groups. **Do this first.**

* **Cost:** nothing, if you already have one. Fifteen minutes to join three groups.
* **Unlocks:** five finished posts in `docs/ACQUISITION_FACEBOOK.md`, aimed at the nearest cohorts.
* **Why first:** it is the only channel where the audience is already gathered by city and by event,
  where a link is often allowed, and where **no follower count is needed**. A brand new Page posting
  into a group gets ignored; a person asking a real question does not.
* **Not a Page.** A RuinMyTrip Page would have zero followers and could not post into groups, which
  is the entire value here. A Page is worth making later, once there is something to point at.

### 2. TikTok, as RuinMyTrip. **The only channel where reach does not need an audience.**

* **Cost:** an account, and somebody filming ten fifteen second videos.
* **Unlocks:** ten scripts in `docs/ACQUISITION_VIDEO.md`, already written, none of which invents a
  traveler.
* **Why second:** every other platform shows your post to people who already follow you. TikTok does
  not, which is the one structural advantage available to a site with no audience at all.
* **Honest caveat:** it needs somebody to film. If nobody will, this drops to fifth and the scripts
  sit where they are.

### 3. Reddit, from an established personal account. **Highest fit, highest barrier.**

* **Cost:** nothing, if such an account exists. **It must not be a new one:** a fresh account posting
  a link is removed by the spam filter, which is exactly what happened to u/TrustMyRecord three times.
* **Unlocks:** ten posts, and the best audience fit of any channel on this list.
* **Blocked from here entirely**, for reading as well as posting, so somebody else has to do it.

### 4. Instagram, as RuinMyTrip. **Only alongside TikTok.**

* Same footage, different caption, so the marginal cost after TikTok is about ten minutes per video.
* On its own it is the weakest of the four, because Instagram reach without followers is close to
  nothing.

### 5. X, as RuinMyTrip. **Last, and optional.**

* X rewards an existing audience more than any other platform here. A new account posting into
  silence produces nothing, and the two posts written for it are worth keeping for when there is
  something to say to people who already follow.

## The recommendation, in one sentence

**One personal Facebook account in three travel groups, and a TikTok account if somebody will film.**
That is two accounts, both maintainable, and between them they unlock fifteen of the twenty finished
posts and all ten video scripts.

Everything else can wait until one of those produces a real signup, which is also the only evidence
that would justify the next account.

## What I will not do

Create any of these, log into any of these, or post from the betting brand accounts. Each of those
is your decision and two of them would risk an account that has nothing to do with this project.
