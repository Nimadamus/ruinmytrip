# Ten short form video scripts

For TikTok, Reels and Shorts. **None has been filmed or posted**, and no RuinMyTrip account exists on
any of those platforms yet.

**Rules for all ten.** No invented traveler stories. No "thousands of travelers". No fake screen
recording showing people who are not there. Where a script shows the product it shows the real
screen, including the honest empty state, because the empty state is the truthful version and it is
also more interesting than a fake full one.

**Footage:** all ten can be shot as a screen recording of the real site plus stock or personal
footage of the city. Nothing needs a presenter on camera.

**Every link is the tracked one.** Platforms strip links from captions, so the link lives in the bio
and the caption says so.

  morning, and a group of six walks into one far more easily than you do.
  You do not need a group. You need one other person. So: post your Munich dates, see which travelers

## 10. Why the dates matter more than the city

* **Hook:** "Everyone asks where. Almost nobody asks when."
* **Script, 25s:**
  "Every travel app on earth will tell you who has been to Rome. That is a fact about the past and it
  helps nobody.
  The useful question is who is in Rome on the 14th. It is a much harder question, because it changes
  every day and it needs both of you to say something first.
  That is the entire idea: a city, two dates, and whose overlap."
* **On screen text:** `Who has been to Rome` crossed out / `Who is in Rome on the 14th`
* **Caption:** Where is the easy question. When is the useful one.
* **CTA:** bio link. **Campaign:** `dates-not-places`

## Posting plan, once an account exists

One video per week, Wednesday, per the weekly plan in `docs/ACQUISITION_WEEKLY.md`. Order: 1, 5, 6,
2, 7, 8, 9, 3, 4, 10, so the nearest cohort goes first and the two honest, non promotional ones
(7 and 2) land early enough to set the account's tone.

**Threshold for the channel as a whole:** 100 views and 2 human visits on any single video, within
the first three. If three videos produce no visits at all, the channel is wrong for this and the
effort moves elsewhere rather than to a fourth video.
