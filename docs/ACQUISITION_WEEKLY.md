# The weekly acquisition plan

Acquisition stops working when it becomes bursts of activity followed by nothing. This is the
repeating week. It is deliberately small: **one published thing a day at most**, because one post
read properly teaches more than five posted at once, and because five at once is how accounts get
removed.

## The week

**Monday: read, then decide.**
Open `/admin/funnel`. The top card is the morning line: human visits, signups, confirmed, trips, for
today and the last seven days, with our own checks excluded. Then the channel table over 24 hours,
7 days and the window.
Write down the best and worst channel of the last week and why. Prepare the week's posts from
`docs/ACQUISITION_PUBLICATIONS.md` and check every link still returns 200.
**No posting on Monday.**

**Tuesday: a destination or community post.**
One post, one group or forum, from the publications list. Read the rules first. Record the row in
`docs/ACQUISITION_EXPERIMENTS.md` before publishing, not after.

**Wednesday: short form video.**
One video from `docs/ACQUISITION_VIDEO.md`, in the order at the bottom of that file.

**Thursday: the cohort post.**
Whichever campaign the calendar says is in its posting window. This is the highest value day because
a dated cohort is the only thing that makes "who else is there" urgent.

**Friday: a traveler question.**
Not about the site. A real question in a real community, answered honestly when people reply. A week
with nothing but links in it reads as a marketing account and gets treated as one.

**Weekend: answer people.**
Reply to anything that came back during the week. Do not post. Do not chase. If somebody signed up,
look at whether they posted a trip, because that is the step that is currently at zero.

## What changes the plan

* **A channel produces a signup:** post there again next week, same day, different text.
* **A channel produces visits and no signups:** the landing page is the problem, not the channel.
  Look at what those visits landed on before writing another post.
* **A channel produces nothing twice:** change the hook once. **Nothing three times: drop it.**
  The channel is the hypothesis, not the wording.
* **A post is removed:** record the reason, do not repost there, do not argue with the moderator.
  That subreddit or group is finished for us and it costs nothing to accept that.

## The rules that do not change

* One post per channel per week.
* Never the same text twice.
* Read the rules immediately before posting.
* Disclose the interest every time.
* No paid promotion, no bought anything, no mass posting, no unsolicited messages.
* Protecting an account is always worth more than a post.

## What "working" looks like, in order

1. **100 human visits.** Currently in the tens, and most of those were our own verification.
2. **25 signups.** Currently 2 in the window.
3. **10 trips with dates.** Currently 1 all time.
4. **Two trips overlapping in one city.** Has never happened, and it is the only one that proves
   the product does what it says.

If four weeks of this produce nothing on step 1, the problem is not the schedule and not the copy.
It is that nobody has a reason to care yet, and the answer is a better cohort rather than more posts.
