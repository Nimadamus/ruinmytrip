# Proposal: ten questions asked by RuinMyTrip, in the open

**Not implemented. This is the proposal you asked for, with the risk stated first.**

## The problem it would solve

A city community with nothing in it gives a visitor nothing to react to. The empty state is honest,
and honest is not the same as useful. Somebody who lands on the Munich page four days before
Oktoberfest and sees no questions has nothing to answer, nothing to disagree with and no reason to
come back.

## The risk, stated plainly

**This is one step away from the thing you told me never to do.** Fake community activity is a line
that does not move, and a question posted by a site pretending to be a traveler is exactly that. The
only version of this worth doing is one where the authorship is unmistakable at a glance, and where
the question is one a real traveler would actually ask.

If there is any doubt at all about whether a reader would know the site asked it, the answer is not
to soften the label. It is not to post it.

## What would make it safe

1. **Attribution in the post itself, not only in the byline.** Every one begins "RuinMyTrip asks:".
2. **Posted by a visibly editorial account**, the same role that owns the destination reviews, shown
   with whatever marker that role already carries. Never a personal looking username.
3. **No fake replies, ever.** Not by us, not by a second account, not "seeded" with an answer.
4. **No counts attached.** The question does not claim anybody is reading it.
5. **Ten, once.** Not a generator, not one per city, not a cron job. Ten questions, written by hand,
   tied to the cohorts we already verified, and then stop and look at what happened.
6. **Deleted if they sit unanswered.** A question with no answers after the event has passed is
   clutter, and leaving it up makes the city look deader than an empty page does.

## The ten, if approved

Each is a real question a traveler asks, and each is on a city where a verified cohort is coming.

1. **Munich.** RuinMyTrip asks: if you have done Oktoberfest without a group, did you find people
   beforehand or sort out a table on the day?
2. **Munich.** RuinMyTrip asks: which days of Wiesn would you tell a first timer to avoid entirely?
3. **Oaxaca.** RuinMyTrip asks: for Dia de Muertos, which cemetery on which night, and which ones are
   better left to the families?
4. **Lisbon.** RuinMyTrip asks: Web Summit fills 9 to 12 November. What is worth doing on the days
   either side?
5. **Chiang Mai.** RuinMyTrip asks: Yi Peng's ticketed release or the free ones in the old city,
   and would you do the same again?
6. **Bangkok.** RuinMyTrip asks: New Year in Bangkok or head north to Chiang Mai. Which would you
   repeat?
7. **Miami.** RuinMyTrip asks: during Art Week, which days are worth being in Wynwood and which are
   the ones locals avoid?
8. **Rio.** RuinMyTrip asks: how do you choose blocos at Carnival when you do not know anybody there?
9. **Amsterdam.** RuinMyTrip asks: what is the one thing you would tell somebody arriving alone for
   a weekend here?
10. **Marrakech.** RuinMyTrip asks: is the Atlas or desert day trip worth it, and does it change if
    you are going on your own?

## What would tell us it worked

* **A real reply from a real traveler.** One is enough to justify the next ten.
* **A search entry point.** These are long tail questions in the exact words people type, on pages
  that already exist. If any of them earns an impression, that is a second reason to continue.
* **Anything shared.** A question is shareable in a way a destination page is not.

**What would tell us to stop:** ten questions and no replies after the cohort has passed. That is
the site talking to itself, and the right response is to delete them and put the effort into
distribution instead.

## What I need from you

A yes or a no to the ten above. **I have not posted any of them and will not without that.** If yes,
I would also want to confirm which account posts them and how the authorship is displayed, because
that is the part that makes it honest rather than the part that makes it work.
