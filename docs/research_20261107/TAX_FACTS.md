## Amsterdam
- Rate: 12.5% of the room charge subject to VAT (so the base excludes VAT and the tourist tax itself). Source: Verordening Toeristenbelasting 2024, CVDR703815, which applies from 1 Jan 2024 and has no later ordinance.
- There is no cap on nights and no age exemption. The accommodation provider is liable and may pass the tax on to the guest.
- VAT on hotel accommodation rises from 9% to 21% from 1 Jan 2026 (Rijksoverheid). Camping stays at 9%.
- 2027: the plan is 16%, then 1 point a year up to 20%. This is **proposed**, not adopted: the council vote is reported for 11-12 Nov 2026. Sources for this are SECONDARY (KHN and news).
- Discrepancies: the calculator tip says "Accommodation VAT … 21%", but camping is still 9%. The post's "roughly a third of the quoted rate" holds only for the net rate. On a price that includes VAT the share is about 28%.

## Barcelona
- From 1 Apr 2026 (ATC and Llei 2/2026), the Barcelona city base rate is: 5* 7.00, 4* 3.40, tourist apartment 4.50, youth hostel 1.00, other 2.00, cruise 4.00 (>12h) / 6.00 (≤12h).
- The city surcharge (Ordenança fiscal 2.2) is 5 EUR for Apr 2026 to Mar 2027, 6 EUR from Apr 2027, 7 EUR from Apr 2028, and 8 EUR after that.
- At most **7 nights per person** are taxed, and people **aged 16 or under are exempt**. The tax is paid through the establishment and shown on the invoice.
- 2027: the surcharge rises to 6 EUR from 1 Apr 2027 (**adopted**).
- Discrepancies:
  1. Calculator: no `cap`, so stays longer than 7 nights are overstated.
  2. Calculator: every person is counted, but those aged 16 or under are exempt.
  3. Post: does not mention the 7-night cap or the age exemption.
  4. Post title: "4.50 euros plus 5 euro city surcharge". 4.50 is only the apartment rate.
  5. Calculator: missing tiers (hostel 6.00 total, other 7.00 total). This is an omission only.

## Lisbon
- 4.00 EUR per guest per night, for at most 7 nights per stay, from 1 Sep 2024. Source: Aviso 18684/2024/2, DR 26-08-2024.
- Exempt: guests under 13, medical stays plus one companion, emergency placements, and incoming university students (up to 60 days).
- The tax is collected by the accommodation, local lodging (AL) operator or a platform. Cruise passengers in transit pay a separate 2 EUR sea-arrival tax.
- 2027: no change found.
- Discrepancy: the post says the cap and the age rule "sit in the wider municipal regulation" and should be checked. The regulation sets 7 nights and under 13.

## Nice
- The official 2026 sheet matches the post exactly, including the 34% LNPCA additional tax and the 6.70% rate for unrated stays capped at 6.43.
- Exempt: minors, seasonal workers, people in emergency rehousing, and rents below 23 EUR/day. There is no cap on nights.
- 2027: the Métropole portal says "TARIFS 2027 … restent identiques aux Tarifs 2026".
- Discrepancies: the post says the exemptions are not stated, but the Métropole's 2026 modalités sheet lists them. The calculator does not say minors are exempt.

## Paris
- The official grid matches the post and the calculator, as does the unrated rule (5% plus the add-ons, capped at 15.93).
- Exempt: minors, seasonal workers, Paris residents, and people in emergency rehousing.
- 2027: the grid stays identical to 2026, "sous réserve de modification législative ultérieure".
- No discrepancies.

## Rome
- The tariffs from 1 Oct 2023 (DGC 255/2023) are still the latest on comune.roma.it and match the post and the calculator.
- At most 10 consecutive nights in the same property per calendar year are taxed (5 at campsites). Children are exempt until their 10th birthday.
- 2027: no change found.
- Discrepancies: the post does not mention the under-10 exemption. The calculator gives the range as "3.50 to 10", but campsites are 3.00, and youth-hostel stays are exempt.
