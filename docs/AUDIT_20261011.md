# RuinMyTrip audit, 2026-10-11

Evidence was pulled 2026-10-11 between 00:00 and 01:30 PDT from production `/cron/funnel`, `/cron/kpi`,
`/cron/places?op=index_quality`, the Search Console API (Search Analytics, Sitemaps, URL Inspection),
the Search Console UI (Manual actions, Page indexing, Crawl stats), live HTTP checks, and a local
dev server walk through the review flow. Nothing was deployed, published or changed in production.
Raw pulls: `%TEMP%\rmtaudit\` (date_page.json, daily.json, query_page.json, insp_out.txt).

## 0. Bottom line

* **Users.** In the last 30 days: 1 registered member, 0 published community reviews, 0 trips,

* **So the place page is the front door**, and the review is the first contribution to win.
  Users this month have to come from direct channels, with the travel map and reviews as the offer.

### 1.2 Why the sitemap change is not the main cause

1. **Timing.** Places, the blog, guides, categories and neighborhoods left the sitemap on 9/21
   (commit 747e11a). The week of 9/21 held at 752 impressions against 807 the week before. The
   cliff came a week later, over three days.
2. **The control fell too.** City pages `/d/*` stayed in the sitemap the whole time and fell 74%
   in the same week (27 to 7). Every section fell by roughly the same share.
3. **Nothing technical removed the pages.** URL Inspection on the seven biggest losers
   (le-rapido-paris, stade-roland-garros-paris, nini-s-coffeebar-amsterdam, stow-bazaar-london,
   pizzeria-ai-marmi-rome, musee-des-arts-decoratifs-paris, zeughauskeller-zurich) and two blog
   posts: all "Submitted and indexed", fetch OK, robots allowed, Google canonical equals our
   canonical. Live HTML returns `index, follow` and a self canonical. Page indexing in the UI:
   **1,140 indexed**, 428 noindex (by our own thin page rule), 42 discovered not indexed,
   3 crawled not indexed, 4 alternate canonical, 2 not found, 1 redirect. No manual action.
4. **Positions held, page count fell.** Average position did not collapse (15 to 21). The
   number of place pages Google chose to show fell from 172 a week to 27. That is a serving
   (quality) demotion, not a ranking slide or deindexing.
   A wider URL Inspection sample (196 URLs, 10/11): **39 of 39** randomly sampled place pages that
   ever earned impressions are still "Submitted and indexed". Of 12 place pages linked from the
   Tokyo and Bangkok city pages that never earned impressions, 7 are unknown to Google, 3 are
   noindexed by our thin rule and 2 are indexed. Of the 85 city pages in the sitemap since 9/08,
   **24 are still unknown to Google**, including Athens and Bangkok. So are /map, /talk and
   /travelers. The /e event guides are "Discovered, not indexed" or unknown.
5. **Google stopped recrawling them.** The lost pages were last crawled 9/13. Crawl stats over
   90 days: 4.65K requests, 95% OK, 385 ms average, "host had problems in the past" with under 1%
   5xx and robots.txt failures. Low crawl demand is a symptom of the same demotion.

### 1.3 Most likely cause: site quality judged during the spam update

Google's September 2026 spam update ran 9/24 to 10/8, with volatility reported 9/25 to 9/27,
9/30 and 10/4 to 10/6. Our cliff is 9/28 to 9/30. The site has the profile spam systems look for
as "scaled content":

* 1,393 places imported from OpenStreetMap. 561 have only a name and a map point (already
  noindexed). 0 places have a photo. 111 have a review, and those are editorial.
* A place page is about 320 words, most of it the same CTA blocks on every page (who is going,
  find travelers on my dates, ask a question, be the first to review).
* 143 templated blog posts ("X tourist tax 2026", "X tickets 2026") and 185 desk research
  "Official Reviews" with star ratings. The reviews are clearly labeled, are not counted in
  community ratings and carry no Review schema, which is correct.

**Confidence: moderate.** Google never confirms a site level spam classification. Other causes
I checked and ruled out: manual action, robots, noindex, canonicals, redirects, 5xx, deindexing.

### 1.4 Restoration plan (reversible, nothing pushed yet)

Do **not** put 1,393 places back in the sitemap. Sequence:

1. **Fix the template first.** Lead each place page with what is unique to it (hours, address,
   how to get there, nearby, questions), cut the repeated CTA blocks to one, and only show the
   buddy block where a city has a real traveler. The change ships behind a flag so it is one
   switch to undo.
2. **Then submit a curated set, separately.** New `sitemap-places.xml`, its own child of the
   index, so it can be removed with one line and measured on its own. Entry rule: three or more
   real signals (410 places qualify) **and** either past impressions (345 distinct place URLs
   earned impressions since August) or a review. Estimated 150 to 250 URLs.
3. **Consolidate rather than delete the blog.** Propose merging the 143 tax and ticket posts
   into one maintained "Tourist taxes and entry fees 2026" dataset page plus per city sections,
   with 301s from each post. This is a URL change, so it is **proposed only**. It also becomes
   the link earning asset for bloggers (section 4).
4. **Measure for 4 to 8 weeks** before judging. Spam update demotions usually lift only after
   Google reprocesses the site.

Conflict to settle once: our standing rule says never noindex, but `app/indexability.php`
noindexes 428 thin pages and that rule is the right thing for quality. Recommend keeping it.

### 2.2 What I tested (local server, same code as production)

Place page, then star, then form with the rating prefilled, then publish as a guest, then
`/plan/join` showing "Your review is ready" with the text, then email, password and 16+ tick.
**The happy path works.** Findings:

1. **A rejected guest submit is invisible.** Guest submits record nothing before `csrf_check()`.
   If the form token is rejected, the visitor gets a bare 403 "Your form session expired. Go back
   and try again." and we record no event. So we cannot tell whether Zurich never pressed publish
   or was rejected.
2. **The draft is erased too early.** `review-draft.js` clears the saved text on submit, before
   the server accepts it. A 403 or a network failure loses everything typed.
3. **The form looks longer than it is.** Trip date, "What was great?" and "What nearly ruined
   the trip?" are optional in the validator but not marked optional. The review needs 40
   characters.
4. **A held review lives only in the PHP session.** If the visitor confirms their email on
   another device, or the session expires, the review is gone. A server side draft with an
   expiry fixes this and is what "save drafts securely" needs.
5. **Email signups publish only after the confirm link.** Google signups publish at once.
6. **Google sign in is live** (`/auth/google` redirects to Google with the client id; one account
   was created that way). Still to verify: the consent screen is in Production, not Testing (in
   Testing, strangers see "Access blocked"), and the Google button shows on `/plan/join`.
   Locally the copy says "With Google it goes live straight away" with no button, because local
   has no client id. Production needs the same check.
7. **Mobile.** The form and place page render cleanly in headless Edge at phone width. Headless
   enforces a minimum window width, so possible right edge clipping is **not verified**. A proper
   device emulation pass is part of the 7 day plan.

### 2.3 Proposed flow (needs review before deploy)

* **Step 1, one screen:** stars (prefilled), one text box, "Post review". Everything else moves
  to an "add detail" step after it is saved.
* **Server side guest draft:** `review_drafts` table, random token in an HttpOnly cookie,
  30 day expiry, deleted on publish. Survives session loss and device switch through the
  confirm link.
* Clear local text only on the success page. Track `review_submit_attempt` for guests and a
  `csrf` failure reason. On a token failure, re-render the form with the text instead of a 403.
* Google button on `/plan/join` beside email, after the consent screen check.

  placement in reviews or ratings, ever.
