## Why the map leads

It is the one feature that is useful to a single stranger today, with zero reviews and nobody else
on the site: tap your countries, get a picture and a link. It also brings people back to the parts
that grow with a community (rate a place, post dates). We stop waiting for 10 reviews before promoting.

Rules for every item: real experiences only, no invented reviews or numbers, no fake engagement, no
cold mass messages, one reply per thread, disclosure wherever the owner links his own site.

## How we judge it

/admin/funnel, "Where people came from", one row per link above: people (the /admin/proof verdict),
map views, maps shared or saved, signups, came back later. Suspected bots and unverified visits stay
in their own columns. Our own checks use `utm_content=selfcheck` and never count as people. A channel
earns a second round only when it brings people who share or save a map.
