> city and dates and see who else will be there at the same time. Nobody can message you until you

### Indie Hackers / r/SideProject
> **I built a travel community where nobody can message you until you accept them**
>
> RuinMyTrip lets you post where you are going and when, and shows you the travelers whose dates
> overlap. Others can raise a hand with a short note; messaging opens only after you accept. Travel
> buddies and meetups are 18+, checked on the server, not just hidden in the interface.
>
> It also has honest reviews (what was great, what ruined it) and a map of where you have been.
>
> Where it stands, honestly: about two visitors a day and no members yet. I would love feedback on
> the first five minutes: https://ruinmytrip.com/?utm_source=indiehackers&utm_campaign=launch&utm_content=founder-post

### Cruise Critic roll call (only where links are allowed)
