# Outreach drafts for the travel buddy pages

Written 2026-09-18 (Pacific). For a person to post from an established account of their own. Nothing
here has been posted: see `docs/ACQUISITION_EXPERIMENTS.md` E9 for why no account on this machine can.

Rules for every one of these:

* Read the community's rules the same day, right before posting. If links or self promotion are not
  allowed, post the no link version and do not add the link in a comment either.
* One post per community. Never the same text in two places on the same day.
* Say plainly that you run or help build RuinMyTrip whenever the site is mentioned.
* Log each post in `docs/ACQUISITION_EXPERIMENTS.md` (where, when in Pacific time, text, link, result).

Tracked links: add `?utm_source=<site>&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=buddy_pages&utm_content=<post id>`.

## D1. r/solotravel, discussion post, no link

**Title:** What is the best way you have found company for one part of a solo trip?

**Body:**
I travel solo most of the time and I like it, but there is always one part of a trip that is better
with somebody: splitting a car in Iceland, a boat day in Thailand, the Inca Trail, a late dinner in
Spain. Hostel common rooms work if you are staying in one. Beyond that it feels like luck.

What has actually worked for you? Apps, Facebook groups, walking tours, just asking at the next
table? And what made you trust somebody enough to share a day with them?

*(No link. If people ask what I am working on, answer in a reply, and only if the sub allows it.)*

## D3. Let's Go Solo (Facebook group), only after the admin says yes

**Post:**
Hey, I help build RuinMyTrip, a free site for finding people going where you are going, on your
dates. The admins okayed this post.

You add a city (or a cruise ship) and your dates, and you see who overlaps with you. Nobody can
message you until you accept, and cards never show a hotel, cabin or address.

It is new, so some places are still empty, and the page says so. If you have a trip coming up,
adding it is how the next person going there finds you:
https://ruinmytrip.com/buddies?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=buddy_pages&utm_content=letsgosolo

Happy to answer anything here.
