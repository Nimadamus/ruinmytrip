## Order, and why

1. **Now:** AlternativeTo, BetaList, the directories. They sit quietly, send a trickle of curious
   people, and each is a backlink to a three month old domain.
2. **After the seeding kit lands about 50 real reviews:** Show HN and Indie Hackers. Readers click
   straight in; an empty site gets one look and no second one.
3. **Last, once there are 100+ reviews and a few trips:** Product Hunt. A launch day happens once.

## Facts every listing may use (all true today)

* Free. Join at 16, posting a trip is 18+.
* Write a review with no account; the account comes at the end and the review is shown back.
* Post trip dates, see who overlaps, mutual opt in before anyone can message you.
* Location is destination and dates only; photos are re-encoded so GPS metadata is removed.
* 85 cities and about 1,400 places with pages. Never quote member or review counts that are not
  read from the site the same day.

## Shared copy

Name: **RuinMyTrip**

Tagline (60 characters max): **Honest trip reviews and people going where you're going**

Short description (260 max):
> RuinMyTrip is a travel community where people review the places they went, good and bad, and
> find travelers heading to the same city on the same dates. Write a review with no account,
> post your dates, and only talk to people who say yes back.

Long description:
> Most review sites are full of five stars nobody believes. RuinMyTrip asks one question first:
> what nearly ruined the trip? Travelers review restaurants, hotels, tours and neighborhoods they
> actually went to, including the price they paid and what they wish they had known.
>
> The same site is how you find people. Post where you are going and when, and you see who else
> will be there on overlapping dates: other travelers, locals open to meeting, people on the same
> cruise. Nobody can message you until you both say yes, and your profile never shows more than
> the city and the dates.
>
> It's free, it's new, and it's built by one traveler who wanted a place where the bad parts get
> said out loud.

> The second half is finding people. If you're going to Lisbon in November, you can see who else
