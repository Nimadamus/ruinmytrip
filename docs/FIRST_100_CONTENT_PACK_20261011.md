# First 100 users: content pack (DRAFT, NOTHING POSTED)

Prepared 2026-10-11 (Pacific). Companion to `FIRST_100_CAMPAIGN_DRAFTS_20261011.md` (rules table,
tracked links, 30 day calendar, stop rules), which still applies. Nothing here has been posted,
scheduled, sent or signed up for. Every item is for the owner to approve, rewrite in his own voice
where a venue bans generated text, and post from his own accounts.

Ground rules for every piece:
- True numbers only. Today: about 35 verified human visitors in 31 days, 1 real member, 0 community
  reviews. Never claim users, reviews, ratings or a community size the site does not have.
- No fake accounts, no staged stories, no bought views, followers or engagement, no engagement pods,
  no asking anyone to upvote.
- Every fact about a city is checked live on the day it is posted (2026 prices, rules and taxes
  change). The city pages on the site carry our researched notes; quote them only after rechecking
  the source they cite.
- No dashes in any published copy.
- Each piece links with its own tag so the site's verified human count shows which one worked:
  `https://ruinmytrip.com/map?utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=launch-map&utm_content=<piece>`
  (city pieces: `/d/<city>?utm_source=...&utm_campaign=launch-city&utm_content=<piece>`).

## What we lead with, and why

1. **The travel map** (`/map`). Works in 30 seconds with no account, makes a picture people want to
   post, and every shared copy carries our address. This is the hook for short video and Instagram.
2. **Useful city pages** (`/d/<city>`, `/p/<place>`). Real places with hours, addresses and
   directions, plus our labelled research on costs and rules. This is the hook for Quora and search.
3. **Honest reviews** ("what nearly ruined the trip"). The ask, once someone has arrived for 1 or 2.
   Lead with it only when talking to friends and to people who already travel a lot.

Order matters: week 1 is friends only (seed 10 real reviews), then public channels.

## TikTok and Instagram Reels (owner's own footage, voice and trips)

Format for all: 9:16, 1080 x 1920, 15 to 30 seconds, captions burned in for sound off viewing, first
words on screen within 1 second. Screen recordings are of the live site on a phone. Post the same
video on both, each with its own link tag (`tiktok` / `instagram`, `video1` ... `video5`).

**Video 1. "My number" (map reveal, about 20 s)**
- 0 to 2 s, on screen: MY NUMBER IS [real count]. On camera: "Every country I have been to."
- 2 to 14 s: screen recording, tapping his real countries on ruinmytrip.com/map, sped up, counter
  climbing.
- 14 to 20 s: the downloaded picture full screen. Voice: "The one I would go back to tomorrow is
  [real country]. Free, no account. What is your number?"
- Caption: What is your number? Mine is [real count]. Map link in bio.
- Pinned comment from the owner: "Made this because I wanted the picture without signing up for
  anything. Tell me what country you would go back to."

**Video 2. "The one I would not repeat" (about 25 s)**
- 0 to 2 s, on screen: THE TRIP THAT WENT WRONG. On camera: "This nearly ruined my trip to [real place]."
- 2 to 18 s: his own photos, the true story in three sentences ([closed attraction / bad hotel /
  scam / missed connection, whatever actually happened]).
- 18 to 25 s: screen recording of writing it on the place's page. Voice: "I put it where the next
  person will find it before they go. No account needed to write one."
- Caption: What nearly ruined your trip? Honest answers only.
- Only if a true story exists. Skip otherwise.

**Video 3. "One good thing, one bad thing" (about 20 s)**
- On screen: HONEST REVIEW: [real place]. Three cuts: one good thing, one bad thing, who it is for.
- Last 5 s: the review going up on the place page.
- Caption: One good thing, one bad thing. That is the whole review.

**Video 4. "Before you book [city]" (about 25 s, uses a city page)**
- On screen: 3 THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE [CITY]. Voice over a scroll of the live city page.
- Three facts from the city page's own research, each rechecked live on the day of posting, each
  with its date on screen ("as of October 2026").
- End: "The rest is on the page, with opening hours for the places people actually go."
- Caption: Saving this for my next trip to [city]? Here is the page.
- Pick a city the owner knows and that Search Console already shows us for (Paris, Amsterdam,
  Stockholm, Zurich, Lisbon are the current ones).

**Video 5. "Who else is going" (about 20 s, only once at least a few real trips are posted)**
- On camera: "Going to [city] in [month]? You can see who overlaps your dates."
- Screen recording of posting his own real dates and seeing the overlap screen.
- Do not post this while nobody else is going; it would show an empty room.

Hashtags (3 to 5, no more): #travelmap #countriesvisited #travel plus the city name where relevant.

## Quora (answers, not posts)

Quora rewards answers that are useful on their own. Each draft below answers the question fully
first; the link is one line at the end with a disclosure. The owner should rewrite each in his own
words (check Quora's current policy on AI assisted answers before posting) and post at most two or
three a week. Never paste the same answer twice.

**Question type 1: "How do I make a map of all the countries I have visited?"**
> There are a few ways. If you only want a picture to share, any visited countries map tool works:
> you tick the countries and download an image. If you want it to stay up to date, pick one that
> lets you save it and come back. Things worth checking: does it need an account (some make you
> sign up before you can see the map), can it show small island countries (many tools make them
> too small to tap), and can you download the picture in a size that looks right on Instagram.
> Disclosure: I built one, ruinmytrip.com/map. It works without an account and the link itself
> stores your countries, so you can bookmark it. [tagged link, utm_source=quora]

**Question type 2: "How do you find a travel buddy safely?"**
> Practical answer from the safety side: meet in public the first time, tell someone where you are,
> keep your own booking and money separate until you know each other, and agree up front on budget,
> pace and what happens if one of you wants to split up. Check that the person's dates and plans
> are consistent and that they have some history somewhere (reviews, posts, mutual friends).
> Disclosure: I run a small site where travelers post their own dates and see who overlaps them.
> It is new, so there are not many people on it yet. [tagged link]
(Only once real trips are posted; until then answer without the link.)

## Other channels worth the time

- **Pinterest.** Pins age well and travel planning is a core use. One pin per city page (a real
  photo plus "Opening hours, directions and honest reviews"), and the map picture. Link tag
  `pinterest`. Low risk, slow burn.
- **Friends and their friends (week 1).** Still the most important: real people writing real
  reviews of places they have been, so strangers arriving later find a site with life in it.
  Draft message in the campaign drafts doc, 3.7.
- **Facebook travel groups.** Most ban links. Only where the group's own rules allow sharing a
  free tool, and only the map.
- **Not recommended:** r/travel, r/digitalnomad, r/travelpartners, r/MapPorn (rules ban it, see the
  rules table), cold DMs, comment spam.

## How we judge it

Same five measures as the campaign drafts doc, per piece: verified humans (the site's own bot
filtered count, an estimate, not a certainty), signups, reviews, map saves, return visits within
7 days. A piece "worked" if it brings at least one review or one map save per 20 verified humans.
Stop a format after three pieces that bring under 10 verified humans together.
