## 2. What is working

* Technically the site is clean: HTTPS, 301 from http and www, robots.txt sane, canonicals self
  referencing, one H1, OG and Twitter cards, share images per object, gzip, immutable asset
  caching, no horizontal overflow on a 390 px phone, CLS near 0 on main pages, LCP 0.3 to 2.8 s.
* Indexing of what Google already knows works: every sampled page that earns impressions is
  indexed with Google agreeing on the canonical.
* Product depth is large: trips with dates and visibility, buddy matching (city, dates, cruise
  line/ship/sailing, locals), communities, talk, Q&A, meetups, reviews with photo EXIF stripping,
  follow cities, notifications, capped email, digest, mutual opt in messaging, block/report,
  moderation, 16+ / 18+ gates, travel map, PWA, funnel instrumentation, 127 green test suites.
* Measurement exists and is honest (human vs automated traffic split, activation stages).

## 3. What is broken

* **Nobody is here.** One real member, zero trips, zero buddy posts, zero traveler reviews. Every
  people page renders an empty state ("Nobody has posted about Vienna yet").
* **Google sees a venue directory, not a travel network.** 98% of impressions and 100% of clicks
  are venue, editorial and tax pages. Not one impression in 90 days for a people-intent query.
* **The people pages are empty templates.** 85 `/d/*/travelers` pages are submitted in the
  sitemap as `index, follow` with no members on them (Vienna: 200 words, all boilerplate, the
  same paragraph on every city). That is the shape Google treats as thin or doorway content,
  and half of them are still unknown to Google weeks after submission.
* **Crawl priority is low.** City pages submitted since August are still "unknown to Google",
  consistent with a near zero authority domain whose crawl budget goes to 1,393 venue pages.
* **Signup friction**: username, email, password, birthdate, then email confirmation before a
  trip can be published. No Google or Apple sign in.
* Fixed in this audit (commit b196f4c, live 2026-10-01): no security headers on HTML responses;
  the destination hero "city" chip rendered as an empty white pill; the header Join button was
  navy text on teal (nav link colour outranked .btn-primary); a test with a hardcoded date had
  turned the gate red.
* Minor: the 470 KB home hero JPEG is served full size to phones; /d/ city pages have 1.5 to 3.1 s
  server time (dynamic PHP on the free plan, no page cache); CLS 0.14 on /map and 0.11 on blog
  posts at desktop width.

## 4. Why Google is not sending traffic

Of the hypotheses A to J, with evidence:

* **A, discovery: partly.** Half the sampled city and travelers pages are unknown to Google.
* **B, discovered but not indexed: partly.** /talk, travelers pages and buddy pages sit in
  "Discovered, currently not indexed": Google chose not to spend a crawl on them.
* **C, indexed but not ranking: yes, the main problem.** Indexed venue/editorial pages rank 9 to
  80 for navigational venue queries, where the user wants the venue's own site or Maps.
* **D, ranks but nobody clicks: yes for the blog and reviews** (positions 9 to 11, 0 clicks).
* **E, wrong searches: yes.** The site's traffic is venue names and tax rates; none of it is a
  person who wants to meet travelers. The 2026-09-21 change that stopped submitting these pages
  was the right call, but nothing people shaped replaced them because there are no people.
* **F, authority: yes, decisive.** New domain (July 2026), no backlinks of note; the head terms
  ("travel buddy Thailand") are held by GAFFL and JoinMyTrip programmatic pages, app stores and
  Tripadvisor threads.
* **G, thin/templated: yes for people pages** (empty templates repeated 85 times).
* **H, internal linking: no.** Hubs link out well; this is not the constraint.
* **I, cannibalization: minor.** /d/{city}, /d/{city}/travelers, /travel-buddies/{country} and
  /in/{country} all target "meet travelers in X" with near identical copy.
* **J: the answer is C + E + F + G together.** SEO cannot rank a social network that has no
  social content. Search is a lagging channel here: it will follow members, not create them.

## 5. Why users would or would not join today

Would: the promise is clear in the first screen ("Find the people going where you are going"),
the product is safe by design (destination and date only, mutual opt in messaging, public
meetups), and posting dates is quick.

Would not: the first thing a visitor sees after the hero is eight questions from "RuinMyTrip
Editorial" with "No replies yet", then "Nobody has posted upcoming dates yet". A buddy finder with
nobody in it gives the first joiner nothing back that day. The value is deferred to an
unspecified future traveler, and the visitor has no reason to believe one will arrive.

## 6. Top 10 highest impact problems

1. Zero members, so every social surface is empty (cold start).
2. No working distribution channel: search sends venue lookups, social accounts are days old,
   Reddit and Cruise Critic were blocked from this machine.
3. Domain authority near zero; no link-worthy asset yet.
4. 85 empty travelers pages submitted for indexing (thin/doorway risk to the whole domain).
5. Indexed footprint is 1,393 venue pages that attract the wrong visitor.
6. The home page leads with unanswered house questions, advertising emptiness.
7. No single focus: 85 cities and 25 country/cruise landing pages split the zero members thinner.
8. Signup has four fields plus email confirmation before the first useful action; no social login.
9. The first joiner in a city gets nothing immediate (no "you are first, here is what happens next"
   with a concrete promise and alert).
10. Product build has outrun usage (103 migrations, 30+ planning docs, 1 member); new features
    will not move the number until people arrive.

## 7. Quick wins that are safe to deploy

Done 2026-10-01 (b196f4c): security headers; hero chip and Join button contrast; red test.

Proposed, need a yes because they touch indexing or the home page:

* **Q1. Submit only travelers pages that have people on them.** Remove empty `/d/*/travelers` from
  sitemap-destinations until a city has at least one public trip, buddy post or local. Pages stay
  live and `index, follow` (no noindex). Low risk, reduces thin URL footprint.
* **Q2. Home page order.** Move "Happening on RuinMyTrip" (house questions, no replies) below the
  buddy search, and hide house questions from the home strip until they have a reply.
* **Q3. Sign in with Google** on /register and /plan (one tap, no password, birthdate asked after).
* **Q4. Responsive hero image** (WebP at 800/1600 px) to cut ~400 KB from the mobile home page.
* **Q5. Page cache for signed out /d/ and /p/ views** (60 s) to bring 1.5 to 3 s server time down.
* **Q6. Close the parameter crawl trap.** robots.txt `Disallow` for `/review/new`, `/login?`,
  `/register?`, `/p/*/correct`, and the `?ask=`, `?sort=`, `?type=`, `?all=` variants of city and
  buddy pages, plus `rel="nofollow"` on the 20,000 per place "write a review" links. Over 5,000 of
  the 8,039 crawled URLs were these. Google stops spending crawl on them and spends it on the
  city pages it has not discovered. No page, canonical or noindex changes.
* **Q7. /in/{country} pages** (56 pages, 71 words each, indexable, not in sitemap): either
  301 to the matching /travel-buddies/{country} page where one exists or give them real content.
  This is a URL change, so it waits for a yes.

## 8. Medium term builds (2 to 8 weeks)

* **One season, one place.** Concentrate all acquisition on one dated event with real demand and
  few people pages: Yi Peng / Loy Krathong, Chiang Mai, 2026-11-24 to 25, then Songkran 2027 and
  Oktoberfest 2027. A dated "who is going" event page with a live list is the format nobody holds.
* **Event pages** (`/e/{event}-{year}`): date range, city, who is going, questions, meetups, and
  an alert for "tell me when someone else posts dates". Index only once one real person is on it.
* **First joiner value**: when you post dates in an empty city you get (a) an alert the moment
  anyone overlaps, (b) your trip page to share with friends, (c) the city's open questions to
  answer, (d) a founding traveler badge. Say this on the page instead of "Nobody yet".
* **Warnings as the search wedge**: first person, dated "what ruined my trip in X" reports with
  price paid and a later "still true?" confirmation. Medium difficulty queries (scams, tourist
  traps, mistakes) where low authority blogs rank. Sourced from members, never fabricated.
* **Trip page as the share loop**: "I'm going to Tokyo Nov 3 to 12, anyone overlapping?" card with
  one tap share to WhatsApp, Instagram story, X, Reddit.
* **Post trip prompt**: "How did Lisbon go? What nearly ruined it?" email 2 days after a trip ends.

## 9. Longer term network effect strategy

Density beats coverage. Win one city per season, then the next, with the same loop: event or
city page, people post dates, overlap alerts bring them back, meetups produce photos and
reviews, reviews and warnings become the indexable long tail, those pages recruit the next
traveler. Expand cities only where at least five real upcoming trips exist. Cruise: target
smaller lines and river cruises where Cruise Critic roll calls are sparse, not big RCL ships.
Trust: lightweight optional proof of trip badge, women only visibility filter, no paywall at
entry (Couchsurfing's paywall emptied its network).

## Competitors and keyword research (summary)

* Head and country buddy terms are owned by GAFFL and JoinMyTrip programmatic pages, app stores
  and listicles: not winnable within 12 months.
* "[ship] [date] roll call" belongs to Cruise Critic and Royal Caribbean Blog.
* Openings: dated "who is going" event pages (Yi Peng, Songkran, Oktoberfest), first person
  warnings (scams, tourist traps, mistakes; competition is low authority blogs), fresh trip
  reports (ranking forum threads are years old), niche and river cruise sailings.
* Hostelworld Social Pass (launched 2025-11-13, 4.99 EUR/week to 59.99 EUR/year) shows people pay
  for traveler chat once density exists; GAFFL charges 6.99 USD per 30 days with ID verification;
  Tourlina uses manual selfie verification; Polarsteps grew on a solo journal loop that gives
  value with zero network.
* No search volumes were available in this session; validate the shortlist in a keyword tool
  before building pages for it.

## Measurement plan

Weekly, one line each: GSC impressions/clicks split brand vs non brand and venue vs people pages;
indexed count from URL Inspection on a fixed 50 URL panel; engaged human visits by source;
signups; activation (signup plus dated trip, or follow plus question, or review/story within 7
days); trips, buddy posts, questions, answers, reviews, comments, follows; overlaps found and
alerts opened; connections accepted and messages sent; WAU and 7/30 day return rate. Sources
already exist in `/cron/funnel`; GSC via `scripts/gsc_report.py`.
