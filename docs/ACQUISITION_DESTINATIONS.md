# The ten destinations worth acquiring into

Not the ten biggest cities. The ten where "who else is here this week" is a question people are
already asking out loud, and where we already have a page that can answer it.

**Evidence base.** Search Console, 90 days to 2026-09-15: **0 clicks, 1,469 impressions, average
position 45.** That average is the important number. Nothing on this site ranks yet, so search is not
going to supply the first hundred people, and a destination's search numbers are used here only as a
tie breaker between cities that are equally good for matching. **No new URL was created for this
list.** Every page below already exists and returns 200.

## How this list was chosen, and what it is not

The filter was: **does a stranger's presence in that city, that week, change what the trip is like?**
That is true of a festival, a conference, a night city and a place where the good thing costs a
vehicle. It is not true of a place people go to be alone, and the largest cities in the world are
mostly on nobody's list here because size is not the same as wanting company.

Berlin, Prague, Budapest and Medellín were considered and left off the ten. All four have pages and
all four fit the nightlife and backpacker pattern; none of them has a dated cohort we can name, which
is what turns a good destination into a campaign. They are the first four to promote when one of the
ten stops earning its place.

## What NOT to do with this list

* Do not create new URLs to fill gaps. Nine of the ten pages already earn impressions or already
  carry a verified cohort; the tenth is Bangkok, which has a cohort and no search presence, and that
  is fine.
* Do not retitle any of these pages. **Lisbon and Oaxaca are in the nine page title experiment** and
  are not to be touched until its first read on 2026-09-29.
* Do not treat the impression numbers as traffic. Zero clicks in ninety days is the whole story: this
  list is about where to send people, not about where they are already arriving from.
