# The first 100 genuine users (plan, 2026-11-07)

Builds on docs/FIRST_USERS_PLAN_20261029.md, docs/LAUNCH_POSTS_20261103.md and the outreach kits.
They are good. Nothing in them has been posted, and that is the only reason the numbers are flat.

## Definitions and baseline

- **Genuine user:** confirmed email, not staff, not a `+rmtqa` or fixture account, and at least one
  public action (a review or warning, a trip with dates, a buddy post, a question or an answer).
  `/cron/members` already counts confirmed genuine members. "Did something" is the funnel's
  `firsts` row on `/admin/funnel`.
- **Baseline** (docs/LAUNCH_POSTS_20261103.md, read from production 2026-11-02): 1 member, 0 member
  reviews, about 60 real visitors in 31 days, 8 Google clicks in October.

Targets below are targets, not forecasts. No conversion rate is assumed, because there is no data
to assume one from. The kill rules decide what stays.

## The one strategic change: lead with the side that works at zero members

A travel buddy finder is worth nothing until there is somebody to find. A warning, a review or a
scam list is worth something to the very first reader. So the first 100 come in through one-sided
value, and the buddy side follows once a few cities have people in them:

1. **"Warn the next traveler"**: one sentence, typed on the homepage, no account until the end.
   The lowest-friction contribution on the site and the brand's whole idea.
2. **Scams and traps** on 8 city pages and **"Trip went wrong? What you can claim"**: sourced pages
   that answer questions people already ask in travel communities. They are the links that can be
   shared without being spam, because they answer the question.
3. **Concentrate on three cities**: Bangkok, Lisbon and Rome. Each has a scam section, editorial
   reviews, places, a buddy page, and a dated reason to go (New Year in Bangkok is already a
   campaign window in `app/acquisition.php`). Ten real people in one city are a community; ten
   spread over 85 cities are ten empty pages.

## Why people come back (what the product now does)

- A helpful vote on a review now reaches its writer ("@ana and 2 others found your review helpful").
- The weekly digest, saved cities, match alerts and the travel map are already built.
- What is still thin: a new member with no trip and no review has little to return to. Next build
  candidate: "Your warning was read by N travelers" on the profile, from a page-view count that
  excludes bots (app/traffic_shape.php already classifies them).
