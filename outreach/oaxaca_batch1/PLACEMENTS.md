## Facebook copy (each group gets its own version; never the same text twice)

**A. Our Page, and groups about Oaxaca travel**

Going to Oaxaca for Day of the Dead this year? The cemetery vigils in Santa Cruz Xoxocotlán are on the nights of 31 October and 1 November, from early evening until morning, and the official 2026 program for the city is not out yet, so parade times you see online are last year's.

We put what is confirmed, what is not, and what travelers wish they had known on one page. You can also add the nights you will be there and get one email when another traveler's dates overlap yours. Free, no account needed.

I help run RuinMyTrip, so tell me if anything on the page is wrong.
[link]

**B. Solo and backpacking Mexico groups**

Anyone heading to Oaxaca solo for Day of the Dead? The cemetery nights run until morning outside the center, which is a lot easier with someone to share the ride back with.

I help run RuinMyTrip, a free site where you add your dates and get told when another traveler will be in the same city the same nights. The Oaxaca page also has the practical stuff: which nights, cemetery etiquette, the cold after dark.
[link]

**C. Digital nomads in Mexico groups**

If you are based in Mexico and thinking about Oaxaca for Muertos: 31 October and 1 November are the cemetery nights in Xoxocotlán, and it is one of the busiest weeks of the year for rooms. We made a page with the checked dates and a free alert for when other people are in town on your dates. Disclosure: I help run RuinMyTrip.
[link]

**D. Day of the Dead groups**

A small thing that catches people every year: the official Oaxaca program for 2026 has not been published yet, so the parade times in most guides are from last year. When it comes out we will update our page. Meanwhile, if you are going and would like to know who else is there on your nights, there is a free alert on it. I help run RuinMyTrip.
[link]
